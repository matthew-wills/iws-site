Everything in this case study is fictional: the operator, the aerodrome, the people and every record. The report is published exactly as the pipeline produced it, faults and all. The grounding audit and the record check below are what the application reported about its own draft, and they are the reason a draft is never final until an investigator has read it.

Report Grounding audit Record check Review notes

The report as drafted

%%REPORT%%

Grounding audit

A second pass over the finished draft against the full record: each claim it could not find, or found contradicted, with the evidence.

%%AUDIT%%

Mechanical record check

No model involved: each factor's category and confidence word in the text compared with its recorded test, the findings compared with the derived classification, and the section lengths against their targets.

%%CHECK%%

Review notes

Observations the model made about the record itself while drafting, kept apart from the prose for the investigator to resolve.
