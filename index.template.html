From evidence to report

Four stages on one record. New evidence can arrive at any point, and the analysis moves with it.

1

Evidence

Capture each item once, whether a document, photograph, recording or interview transcript, and link it to every object it bears on. Interview speakers are mapped to roles, so names stay out of the record.

2

Analysis

Put every event and condition that might have played a part on the AcciMap, including the hypotheses you expect to fail. An excluded hypothesis stays on the map, struck through. An arrow stays grey and dashed until the object it comes from has passed its tests.

3

Assessment

Test each object for Existence, Influence and Importance. Record the argument for, the argument against and your reasoning, with a confidence on a ten-term probability scale. The results decide how the object is classified; the judgements behind them stay yours.

4

Reporting

Export the Evidence and Argument Tables, then build the reports from the same record: a preliminary report, the final investigation report and an executive briefing, written to Word with Professional. Write them yourself under each report's headings, or use optional AI drafting and check the draft against the record.

Optional AI drafting

AI can draft the report from the record. It does not investigate.

How drafts are checked, and where data goes

  1. Draft. A model drafts each section from the record, instructed to use nothing else.
  2. Check. A second model pass flags claims the record does not support, and a mechanical check compares every classification and confidence word with its test.
  3. Decide. You correct and finalise. The report is yours.
