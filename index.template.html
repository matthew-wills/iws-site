Safety investigation software for Windows

A structured workflow for safety investigations.

Investigation Workflow Suite (IWS) takes an investigation from the evidence through the analysis and assessment to the report, in one connected record. You develop the hypotheses and test them against the evidence. IWS keeps the reasoning with every finding and produces the documents.

Download See the case studies

Every investigation is built on an AcciMap

The occurrence sits at the bottom. Above it, in lanes from the people involved up to the regulator, go the events and conditions that might have led to it. Each one is tested against the evidence, and the map shows the result.

One record, from the first evidence to the final report

Evidence, hypotheses and assessments remain connected throughout the investigation. As events and conditions are tested, the supporting reasoning is recorded against each AcciMap object and carried directly into the Evidence and Argument Tables.

Four stages, one record

  1. 1Evidence

    Organise documents, interviews, records and other material in one investigation.

  2. 2Analysis

    Develop and connect hypotheses on the AcciMap as the investigation progresses.

  3. 3Assessment

    Test each event or condition against the evidence and record the reasoning.

  4. 4Reporting

    Generate Evidence and Argument Tables and investigation reports from the same record.
