Investigation work is fragmented. The reasoning shouldn't be.

Investigations are often assembled across documents, folders, spreadsheets, diagrams and SMS records. The evidence may be retained, but the reasoning connecting it to the findings often isn't.

What gets lost
  1. Evidence collected twice, or missed
  2. Findings disconnected from their evidence
  3. New information creates rework
  4. Reviewers can't see how conclusions were reached
  5. Investigation knowledge leaves with the investigator

  • Notes in Word or OneNote

How IWS works

From evidence to report. One connected record.

1Collect
2Build
3Analyse
4Report

Collect the evidence.

Record each item once, with its source, and connect it to the events, hypotheses and findings that rely on it.

Develop the timeline and the causal map.

Lay out the events in sequence and put every line of enquiry on the map, each linked directly to its evidence.

Complete the analysis until the map is finalised.

Test each proposition against the evidence and retain the reasoning. The map is finished when every line of enquiry has been resolved.

Draft the report.

Assemble or draft the report from the same evidence, analysis and findings used throughout the investigation.

Built to stand up to review

The conclusion isn't a black box.

The evidence, tests and reasoning behind every finding remain part of the investigation record.

Every finding has a trail

Trace a finding back through the tests and reasoning to the evidence that supports it. The Evidence and Argument Tables set out the arguments for and against each factor, in Free and as an exported document.

The investigator stays in control

IWS structures the analysis. It doesn't make the judgement. Every verdict, confidence assessment and final conclusion remains yours.

The file stays yours

One readable file on your own drive, readable without IWS and ready to archive when the investigation closes.

Optional AI-assisted drafting

A model can draft the report. It does not investigate.

When enabled, AI drafts from the investigation record under strict grounding rules. A second pass checks the draft against the record before the investigator reviews, corrects and finalises it.

Drafting works well because the investigation is already structured in IWS: the evidence, analysis, reasoning and findings are connected before a model sees any of it.

Your investigation data. Your AI. Your choice. AI-assisted drafting is part of Professional, and you connect the AI: your existing chat workflow, a local model, or an approved organisational API. IWS does not sell AI, and sends nothing anywhere unless you choose to.
