Before you install

The first launch of a new build can trigger a Windows SmartScreen prompt. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

What leaves the computer

  • No AI. Nothing about an investigation leaves the computer.
  • Copy and paste. IWS builds a prompt for you to paste into a chat tool yourself, and you paste the reply back. IWS itself sends nothing.
  • Connected AI. When you draft, IWS sends the record behind the draft to the model service you configured, and nowhere else.

2Questions

The trial and pricing, in plain terms

Is there a trial?

Yes: everything in Professional, once per email address. The trial length is to be confirmed. IWS offers it on one quiet line the first time it runs, and Settings > General > Licence offers it again. Starting it needs the computer online once.

A trial request sends your name, your email address, the edition and a randomly generated installation identifier, which is not a hardware serial number or hardware fingerprint. The licence server also records the IP address the request comes from. Nothing about any investigation is sent.

What happens when a licence ends?

IWS warns you before a licence ends: a notice at start-up 30, 14 and 7 days before, then every day in the last week. A trial warns from 14 days before its end. The licence is checked on the computer itself, so the warnings need no network connection.

A paid, Organisation or Education licence keeps working for 14 days after its expiry date, so a renewal that arrives late does not interrupt an investigation. A trial has no such period. A licence that ends while you are working changes nothing until you close IWS.

After that, IWS opens as Free. Every investigation can still be opened, read and exported, and nothing is deleted. Creating, editing and saving wait until a licence is active.

What stays free?

Reading your own work, with no time limit. Without a licence IWS opens any investigation, shows everything in it and exports the Evidence and Argument Tables and the working record. A licence decides what you can do in IWS, never whether you can open and read your own work. The investigation file is plain JSON, so it can be read without IWS as well.

When can I buy Professional?

Not just yet, and the price is still to be announced. IWS is actively being developed, and we'd like to understand how much interest there is, and what support Professional users will need, before we settle on a price.

In the meantime, the trial gives you everything in Professional. If you'd like to hear when Professional is available, or tell us how you'd use it, we'd be glad to hear from you.

How is a licence activated?

Paste the licence key into Settings > General > Licence. The key is checked on the machine itself, with no account to create and no network needed.

Can I add a licence later?

Yes. Apply a licence at any time and its features become available in the investigations you already have. Changing level writes nothing to an investigation.
