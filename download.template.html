2Questions

The trial and buying, in plain terms

Is there a trial?

Yes: everything in Professional, for a limited time, once per email address. IWS offers it on one quiet line the first time it runs, and Settings > General > Licence offers it again. Starting it needs the computer online once; offline, keep working in Free and start it later.

A trial request sends your name, your email address, the edition and a randomly generated installation identifier, which is not a hardware serial number or hardware fingerprint. The licence server also records the IP address the request comes from. Nothing about any investigation is sent.

How long these details are kept.

What happens when a licence ends?

A paid licence keeps working for 14 days after its expiry date, so a renewal that arrives late does not interrupt an investigation. A trial has no such period.

After that, IWS carries on as Free. Every investigation stays open and editable. Professional-only actions, such as drafting and report export, pause until a licence is active. Nothing is deleted.

What stays free?

Everything in the Free column, with no time limit: the whole investigation workspace, including the AcciMap and the Evidence and Argument Tables, with their exports. A licence decides which features are available, never whether you can open your own work.

How is a licence activated?

Paste the licence key into Settings > General > Licence. The key is checked on the machine itself, with no account to create and no network needed.

Can I start in Free and upgrade later?

Yes. Apply a licence at any time and the Professional actions become available in the investigations you already have. The level changing writes nothing to an investigation.

3Students and educators

The training edition

The training edition is a separate build of the same application, for coursework. It sets IWS up with the method and terms used in transport safety investigation courses, so what a student sees on screen matches what they are taught.

  • A new investigation starts with the course's map name and lane names, in place of the AcciMap and its lanes.
  • The final-report layout modelled on published transport safety investigation reports is first among the report styles.
  • Everything else is as in the standard build, including the levels above: Free until a licence is applied.
