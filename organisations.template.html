For organisations

A more consistent approach to investigations, without compromising professional judgement.

For safety managers, investigation managers and the organisations they work for. IWS gives every investigation the same structure and the same kind of record, whoever conducts it. The investigator still decides what the evidence shows. IWS keeps that reasoning connected to the evidence and the findings, so it can be reviewed.

1Investigation before reporting

The report should emerge from the investigation

In many organisations the report is where the investigation happens. Evidence is summarised into a Word document as it arrives, hypotheses are argued in draft paragraphs, and the reasoning that connects the evidence to a finding ends up spread across versions, emails and the investigator's memory. A reviewer sees the conclusion, but has to reconstruct how it was reached.

IWS keeps that work in the investigation record instead. Evidence is captured once and linked to the events and conditions it bears on. Each hypothesis is tested, and its argument is written down where the test is made. The report is drafted from the record once the analysis is done, so it states what the analysis supports.

2What it gives an organisation

Four benefits for the people who manage investigations

Consistency of process

IWS provides a common investigative method, helping investigators follow a consistent process while accommodating the requirements of individual investigations. Hypotheses are placed on the AcciMap and tested for Existence, then Influence, then Importance where it applies, with a result and a probability recorded for each test. Investigators of differing experience work through the same questions, giving managers a shared standard to review against.

Transparency and traceability

A finding stays connected to its tests, the arguments for and against, and the evidence behind them. The Evidence and Argument Tables set this out object by object, so a reviewer can trace each finding to its support, or see where that support is thin.

Less administration, more investigation

Evidence is captured once and used wherever it applies. The Evidence and Argument Tables and the reports are generated from the record rather than assembled by hand, so investigators spend less time on documents and more on gathering evidence and analysing it.

Reasoning kept with the record

The record keeps why each hypothesis was accepted, excluded or left open, with a dated log of the decisions made along the way. If a finding is questioned months later, or the investigation changes hands, the reasoning is still there to read.

3Gaps and uncertainty

The structure makes gaps easier to see

A structured workflow can't make up for evidence that wasn't gathered, and IWS doesn't decide what caused an occurrence. What it does is make the state of the analysis plain. An untested hypothesis stays a hypothesis on the map. A test the evidence can't settle is recorded as inconclusive. An arrow appears only between objects that have passed their tests, and each result carries the probability the investigator assigned, so a weakly supported finding reads as one.

IWS does not replace investigative judgement or independent review. Its role is to make the evidence, reasoning and uncertainty behind each assessment visible.

Investigation progress at a glance

  • Amber: not yet finalised
  • Green: finalised by the investigator

Each object on the AcciMap displays its finalisation status. Safety managers and reviewers can quickly identify where work remains, then open individual objects to examine the evidence, assessments and reasoning behind them.

This is particularly useful during an investigation handover. An incoming investigator can see which areas need further attention while retaining the reasoning behind work already completed.

Finalisation is recorded by the investigator, independently of the assessment outcome. The Dashboard also provides a count of finalised objects, offering a simple overview of progress without maintaining a separate tracking document.

4Organisation editions

Adapted to your organisation's investigation framework

Where an organisation has its own investigation framework, IWS can be supplied as that organisation's own build, with its framework, terminology and reporting built in as an organisation pack. Each edition is scoped with the organisation, and what it contains depends on what the organisation uses.

Organisation editions retain IWS's desktop-based approach, with investigation files stored and managed within your organisation's existing systems.

The Organisation level alongside Free and Professional →

An organisation pack can set

  • The AcciMap's lanes and the example prompts in each
  • The evidence categories offered when filing evidence
  • The starting list of reasons for the Influence test
  • Default templates and the report styles offered
  • The strongest AI access its investigators can choose
  • Where needed, a findings map built on the organisation's own taxonomy

IWS for investigation training

Students can use IWS Free, which provides the complete investigation workflow with no time limit. Universities and investigation training providers can discuss a customised package reflecting their course terminology, investigation methodology, AcciMap lanes and templates.

A training package is a kind of organisation edition, scoped with each provider around what its course teaches, rather than a standard package offered to every course.
