What IWS does, stage by stage

IWS holds the whole investigation in one record: the checklist, the evidence, the interviews, the timeline, the AcciMap, the tests and the reports, in a single file on the drive you choose. Each stage below works on that same record.

1Evidence 2Analysis 3Assessment 4Reporting Around the investigation Free and Professional
1Evidence

Capture each item once, and link it to everything it supports

Paste a screenshot or text, or drop in a file. Each item records where it came from and which objects on the AcciMap rest on it, so if a source proves unreliable, what needs revisiting is already listed.

2Analysis

Every line of enquiry on the AcciMap

Start with the critical event, then add every event and condition that might have played a part, including the hypotheses you expect to fail. Objects sit in time from left to right and in organisational depth from bottom to top, across six lanes.

  • External Influences
  • Organisational Influences
  • Risk Controls
  • Local Conditions
  • Individual Actions
  • Occurrence Events
3Assessment

Test each object against the evidence

  • Three tests. Did it exist, did it influence the occurrence, and if not, is it still important enough to raise?
  • Argued where it needs to be. Where the evidence cuts both ways, record the argument for, the argument against and your reasoning.
  • Stated confidence. Each result carries a term from a ten-term probability scale, and a pass below the standard of proof is flagged. The standard is 70 per cent by default, chosen in Settings from 50 to 95 per cent.
  • Derived findings. The results decide how each object is classified. An arrow turns solid green only once its source has passed.

4Reporting

Four documents from the one record

The Evidence and Argument Tables export at every level. With Professional, the Record tab assembles the record under a report's headings and every report is written to Word, on your own template if you have one. AI drafting is optional: the Draft report tab drafts each section from the record and checks it against the record before you finalise it.

Evidence and Argument Tables

Each object's tests, with the argument for, the argument against and the reasoning.

Preliminary investigation report

What is known while the investigation runs. No findings.

Final investigation report

The full report: summary, occurrence, analysis, findings and appendices.

Executive briefing

The occurrence, the findings and the actions in a few pages.

The final report also comes in ATSB-, NTSB- and AAIB-inspired layouts, each following that body's published report structure. They are not produced, reviewed or endorsed by those organisations. Read them in Tarlton Springs.

Around the investigation

  • Dashboard. The checklist, one to-do list fed from the checklist and from objects on the map, and the distribution list that prints at the foot of every report.
  • Log. Your running sheet, plus entries the application adds when a decision is made: a checklist answer, a test result, a finalised interview, a draft or an export.
  • One file. No account and no database. The investigation file is JSON, readable without the application.
  • Shared-folder protection. A save that would overwrite a change made on another machine is held for you to decide.
