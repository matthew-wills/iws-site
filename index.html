The problem

The work is spread across a dozen tools, and the reasoning lives in your head

Notes in one application, evidence in a folder, interviews in another, a diagram drawn by hand, and a report written at the end from memory of all of it. Nothing holds the thread between a piece of evidence and the finding that rests on it.

So the record and the report drift apart. A reviewer who asks why one factor was called contributing, or why another was dropped, gets the answer from the investigator rather than from the file. When the investigator moves on, the reasoning goes with them.

What that costs
  • 1Evidence collected twice, or not at all, because no single list says what is outstanding.
  • 2A finding stated more strongly than the evidence behind it ever supported.
  • 3Late information that forces the report to be rewritten from the top.
  • 4A review that cannot check the working, because the working was never written down.
How it works

Collecting and examining evidence is not a straight line, so the workflow does not pretend it is

Evidence arrives when it arrives. A recorder download lands a week in, an interview changes what an earlier statement meant, a maintenance record turns up after the analysis looked settled. The investigation has to stay flexible as new information comes in, and that is what this application is for.

You are notified, you decide an investigation is required, and the checklist starts with whatever is on hand. From there the work runs in rounds: what has come in is recorded, it is analysed, the gaps it exposes become requests for more information, the picture on the map is built out, the evidence behind each object is tested, and round it goes. The report is drawn off the loop when the picture holds, rather than at the point in a sequence where a method says it is due.

Centralised

One to-do list and one evidence store for the whole investigation. A to-do written on an object's form is the same list the Checklist tab shows, with a jump back to the object it came from, so what is outstanding is visible at any point. A capture pasted into an object is held once and appears in the Evidence tab grouped by what rests on it.

How a band may be worded

Every confidence and every finding is recorded as one of these ten bands, so two investigations can be compared. The prose does not have to repeat the same ten words: each band accepts a short list of phrases that state that band and nothing else, and the checks read any of them back as the band it belongs to.

AI drafting

AI drafting is optional. When it is on, a pipeline drafts the report without exaggerating or inventing, audited against the analysis, finalised by you

The model is sent the whole record, with roles in place of names, and drafts each section under grounding rules. A second pass audits the prose against the record and reports every claim it cannot find there. A mechanical check then compares each factor's category and confidence word against the test that produced it. What reaches you is a first draft with its own errors already marked.

You read it against the record, correct what is wrong or overstated, and finalise it. The report that gets published is yours, not the model's.

The checks are there because a model left to itself writes a plausible report rather than a true one. It fills gaps from general knowledge, rounds "likely" up to "virtually certain", and writes a factor that failed its Influence test back in as contributing. None of that shows in the prose, which is the difficulty: the errors read exactly like the truths.

What goes wrong without control
  • 1Facts and figures invented to fill a gap, in the same confident voice as the real ones.
  • 2Confidence overstated: "very likely" becomes "virtually certain", and an argued test becomes a settled fact.
  • 3Evidence reweighed: a factor the investigator excluded is quietly written back in as contributing.
  • 4Recommendations the investigation never made, because reports usually end with some.
  • 5A protected report quoted as if it were an ordinary statement.
  • 6People named where a role was all the report needed.
  • 7Investigation material sent to a service that keeps it, trains on it, or is outside the organisation's control.
  • 8No record of which model, which prompt, or which version of the evidence produced the text.

The drafting pipeline

Run the same way whichever model is behind it. Each stage exists to catch one of the failures above.

The whole record, never a fragment

Every object with its tests and confidences, the timeline, the finalised interviews and the evidence, with roles in place of names.

Grounding rules on every prompt

Nothing that is not in the record, no invented figures, no recommendation the record does not carry, and the confidence word the test actually produced.

Audited, then checked

A pass reports every claim it cannot find in the record. A mechanical check then compares each factor's category and confidence word against its test.

Provenance and staleness

Every draft records the model and the version of the record it came from, and is marked out of date when the record changes.

Local

Which model, and where your data goes

Five things worth settling before drafting is switched on. The user guide works through each of them, with what to ask your IT department for.

One setting, called AI access, decides how every Draft action in the application reaches a model. No AI means none is involved anywhere: reports are assembled from the record, and nothing about the investigation leaves the machine. Chat means the application builds the prompt and copies it out for you to paste into a chat tool you are entitled to use, and makes no network call of its own. Endpoint means it sends the prompt itself, to a model server you have configured, and that is the only setting at which it sends anything anywhere.

An endpoint is a named profile for one model server. It asks for a base URL, a drafting model and an audit model, a protocol, an auth header, a key, and how much of each safety factor to send. The protocol is either the OpenAI-compatible shape, which posts to /chat/completions and is what LM Studio, Ollama and most corporate gateways expose, or the Anthropic Messages API, which posts to /messages. A key is stored on that machine alone: it never enters the investigation file, never reaches an export, and is stripped out of any error shown on screen.

A drafting run sends the whole collated record, with people referred to by role rather than by name throughout, because the role replaces the name when an interview or transcript is brought in rather than at the point of drafting. It goes to the endpoint you configured and nowhere else. The application has no service of its own, phones nothing home, and sends no telemetry.

A consumer chat service such as ChatGPT, Claude.ai or Gemini is the wrong place for investigation material. Those accounts sit outside your organisation's data agreements, the content can be retained or reviewed, and you cannot say afterwards where it went. The application is not designed to be pointed at one; the endpoint route expects an API you or your organisation controls.

That leaves three sensible choices. A local model under LM Studio or Ollama on the investigator's own machine, where nothing leaves the computer and the cost is the hardware you already have, at the price of a smaller model that holds less of the record. Your organisation's private cloud endpoint, on AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex AI or a gateway in front of one, where the material stays inside the organisation's own tenancy and agreement and the models are the strongest available, metered per call. Or your organisation's own hosted model on its own servers, as contained as a local model with no per-call charge, with quality and context limits that depend entirely on what it chose to host.

Connecting a model, in the user guide

The analysis stays live

A late recorder download, a second interview, a maintenance record that turns up in week three: the new information goes into the record, the objects it bears on are retested, and the analysis adjusts around it. A new report is then drafted from the adjusted record in one pass. Nothing starts again from a blank page, and the drafts built on the old record are marked out of date rather than quietly left standing.
