Before you install

The first launch of a new build can trigger a Windows SmartScreen prompt. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

IWS sends nothing about an investigation anywhere unless AI access is set to Endpoint, and then only to the server you configured. The endpoint settings and the drafting controls say where an action would send investigation text off the machine. Starting the Professional trial: trial activation sends your name, email address, the requested edition and a randomly generated installation identifier. The installation identifier is generated by IWS and is not a hardware serial number or hardware fingerprint. Nothing about any investigation is sent.

The trial and buying, in plain terms

What does the 90-day trial include, and how does it start?

Everything in Professional, for 90 days. IWS asks on first run whether to start it, and starting it needs the computer online once. Offline, IWS works as Free and the trial can be started later.

What happens when it ends?

IWS carries on as Free. Every investigation stays open and editable. Professional-only actions, such as drafting and report export, pause until a licence is active. Nothing is deleted.

What stays free?

Everything in the Free column, with no time limit: the whole investigation workspace, including the AcciMap and the Evidence and Argument Tables, with their exports. A licence decides which features are available, never whether you can open your own work.

How do I buy Professional?

A$249 a year per user, the introductory founding price. Talk to us: purchases are handled by hand for now, and you receive a licence key.

How is a licence activated?

Paste the licence key into Settings > General > Licence. The key is checked on the machine itself, with no account to create.
